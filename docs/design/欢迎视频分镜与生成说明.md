## 分段视频提示词

统一约束：same exact approved character identity, black rounded square glasses, long layered dark hair, a single silver-white inner ear streak on her own LEFT / viewer RIGHT, black cable-knit sweater, half-body portrait, full crown and fingers inside frame, clean ivory background, faint flowing blush-pink stage illumination, premium restrained mood, no text, no UI, no new accessories, no actual weapon. Maintain face, spectacles, hairstyle and natural hand anatomy in every frame. Avoid exaggerated smiles, fast camera motion, flicker, morphing or extra fingers.

### A：开场约 5 秒，自动播放一次

首帧 01，末帧 02，03 作为 wink 参考。

The character begins with both eyes gently closed. Hold briefly, slowly open both eyes while keeping her head pose and glasses steady. Look at the viewer, wink her own RIGHT eye once with a tiny confident closed-mouth smile, then return to both eyes open. Extremely subtle camera push. Arms remain down. No jump cuts, no face changes. Finish in the exact relaxed open-eye pose of the ending reference.

### B：静息循环约 4 秒

首尾都以 02 作为目标。

Both eyes open, calm aloof expression. Very subtle natural breathing in shoulders and chest, tiny coherent movement of loose hair, softly drifting blush-pink illumination. Locked camera and matching beginning/end pose for a seamless loop. No repeated wink, no lip movement or hand gestures.

### C：手势约 5–6 秒，供滚动控制

起始为 02，关键姿态为 04、05，终帧为 06 v2。

The woman's own LEFT arm rises from the viewer-right shoulder. Form a natural finger-gun gesture: one extended index finger, raised thumb, other three fingers curled. First point toward her own left temple without touching the face or hiding the glasses. Then sweep the same hand smoothly through a semicircular arc above the crown, then lower the same left forearm across the upper chest below the chin, finally pointing diagonally toward the image upper-left empty space. Her gaze gently follows the fingertip. Camera gradually widens to preserve all fingers and crown, the character shifts slightly right to leave text space on the left. End still and composed. Right arm stays down throughout. Continuous anatomically plausible movement, no mirrored arm swap, no extra hand.

### D：最终姿态静息约 4 秒

以 06 v2 首尾姿态循环。维持左手位置与指向，仅微弱呼吸。网页在指向位置独立绘制 MY WORLD 和 EXPLORE，不在生成视频中加入文字。
